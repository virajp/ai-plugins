# Design — Lovable

Prompt-to-app at lovable.dev. Not a canvas: what comes back is generated
application code, and the import reads screen structure out of it.

**That makes the screens import lossier than a canvas's**, and the pack says
exactly where rather than presenting the payload as equivalent. Design system
and conversations are both real surfaces; the conversations skill records why
one obvious substitute is deliberately not used.

The slug is the `projects.<name>.design` token itself.
